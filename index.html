THE SPEC SHEET

Why it holds up

Fabric first

We buy woven and knit cotton by the bolt from mills we've used for years — not by the season's trend forecast. Heavier grams, tighter weaves, fewer thin spots by month three.

Stitched to work

Bar tacks at every stress point, felled seams on anything that'll take a load, and a stitch count that's overbuilt on purpose. If a seam gives, we fix it free the first year.

Cut to move

Gusseted underarms, articulated knees, and a torso that doesn't ride up when you reach overhead. Sized for the body you actually have on a work day, not a lookbook.

CARE NOTES, NO. 12

Wash cold. Hang dry. It'll outlast the dryer anyway.

Heavyweight cotton doesn't need coddling, just consistency. Turn garments inside out, wash cold with like colors, and skip the dryer on anything with a print or wax finish — air gets it there just as fast and keeps the fibers long.
